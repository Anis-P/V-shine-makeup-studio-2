Our Story

Meet Vishakha Bangare

Welcome to V Shine Makeup Studio & Family Salon – where beauty meets artistry and every client leaves feeling like their most radiant self.

Founded in 2012 by the passionate and talented Vishakha Bangare, V Shine has grown from a small home-based studio into one of the most sought-after premium makeup studios and beauty academies in India.

With over 12 years of experience, thousands of happy brides, and a team of 25+ expert artists, we take pride in creating personalized beauty experiences that celebrate your unique features and personality.

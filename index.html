FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions about our services and booking process.

How do I book an appointment?
You can book an appointment by calling us at 9987936131, sending a WhatsApp message, or filling out the contact form on our website. We recommend booking at least 2 weeks in advance for bridal services.
Do you provide on-site/destination wedding services?
Yes! We provide on-site makeup services for weddings and events across India. Travel charges may apply depending on the location. Contact us for destination wedding packages.
What products do you use for makeup?
We use only premium international brands including MAC Cosmetics, Huda Beauty, Charlotte Tilbury, NARS, Bobbi Brown, Fenty Beauty, and more. All products are authentic and skin-friendly.
Is a trial session included in bridal packages?
Trial sessions are included in our Diva Package and can be added to other packages for an additional fee of ₹3,000. We highly recommend trials for brides to ensure we achieve their desired look perfectly.
What is your cancellation policy?
Cancellations made 7 days before the appointment receive a full refund. Cancellations within 3-7 days receive 50% refund. No refunds for cancellations within 48 hours. Rescheduling is possible based on availability.
